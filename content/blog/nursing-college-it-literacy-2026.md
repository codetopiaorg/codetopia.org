In April 2026, Codetopia Community and **Becc Academy** ran an IT literacy workshop at the **Nursing and Midwifery Training College, Koforidua**.

Tech isn't only for people who write code. Nurses and midwives share health information every day, and a clear poster can carry a message further than a long talk. So the workshop was about design, for health.

## What the students learned

- **Create health posters:** design effective posters quickly, using Canva's features and templates.
- **Speed up design:** use Canva's AI tools to make the design process faster and simpler.
- **Communicate clearly:** put a health message across visually, so it is understood at a glance.
