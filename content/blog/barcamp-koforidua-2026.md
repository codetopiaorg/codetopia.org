On Saturday 14 November, Codetopia Academy was at **BarCamp Koforidua 2026**, held at SGS, Koforidua Technical University. The theme this year was *Environment, Finance and Impact*, and the day was all about learning, sharing, networking and mentoring.

Juliet Asiedu, administrator at Codetopia Academy, was one of the mentors. Her topic was a question many people ask themselves:

> What's your background, and how can technology make you better at what you do?

## Not just for coders

A lot of people think tech is only for people who write code. Juliet's point was the opposite. Whether you are in finance, farming, teaching, health or trade, there is a tool or a skill that can make your work faster, clearer or easier to grow.

She talked with students about:

- starting from what you already know, not from scratch
- picking one small tool and getting good at it
- asking questions early, and finding people to learn with
