On 21 and 22 February 2025, Codetopia Community supported **Django Girls Koforidua** at the **Koforidua Library**.

Django Girls workshops help women take their first steps in programming by building a website with Django, the Python web framework. Over two days, participants worked through practical Django sessions with mentors beside them.

## What happened

- **Building real projects:** participants built their own Django projects, step by step.
- **Deploying them:** they put their work online, so it is something they can show.
- **Learning together:** mentors guided the problem solving, and participants helped each other along the way.

The goal was confidence as much as code: leaving the room knowing you can build something, and knowing where to go next.

## Thank you

Thank you to the Django Girls Koforidua organisers, the mentors, and every participant who spent their weekend building.
