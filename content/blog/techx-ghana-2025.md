On 16 July 2025, Codetopia Community joined **IEEE CS SYP TechX Ghana**, hosted with the IEEE GCTU Student Branch on the GCTU campus.

Our session was about **Django**, the Python web framework. Instead of slides alone, we did a live demo: building with Django on stage to show what the framework gives you out of the box.

## Why a live demo

Watching something get built answers questions that slides can't. Students could see how a Django project is set up, how its parts fit together, and how quickly you get from an idea to something that works.
